Where Can I Sit?

A little rest, closer than you think.

Every journey deserves a seat. Where Can I Sit? is an interactive map-based prototype created to help pedestrians, commuters, seniors, and parents locate quiet, welcoming public seating nearby. Instead of simply plotting bench coordinates, it focuses on real-world human comfort criteria.

🔍 Why those specific details matter:

They allow people to decide whether a place truly meets their physical needs before they expend energy travelling there:

How Machines Find Patterns in Data

One of the concepts I’ve loved exploring is K-means clustering. Without any human supervision or labels, the algorithm partitions N observations into K distinct clusters based on mathematical proximity.

  • 01
    Centroid Initialization: Select K initial cluster centers at random.
  • 02
    Assignment Step: Calculate Euclidean distance and assign each point to its nearest centroid.
  • 03
    Update Step: Move each centroid to the geometric mean of all points assigned to it. Repeat until convergence!
💡 Why this inspires me: Simple distance formulas give rise to emergent spatial intelligence.
